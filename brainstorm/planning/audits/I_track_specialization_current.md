# Audit D — Track-specialization review

The event’s track labels are intentionally broad, so a candidate cannot rely on a topical noun alone. Track relevance is a named judging axis. A strong track case therefore answers: what outcome in this track becomes better only because this product exists?

## Findings

| Track | Strongest retained track proof | Cosmetic-fit failure to reject |
|---|---|---|
| Optimization | A visible objective/constraint change produces a measurably better feasible allocation, repair, or cycle. | Calling an AI workflow optimization because it recommends something. |
| Traveling | A traveler makes or changes a real movement/disruption/accessibility decision. | A generic chatbot, translation tool, or social app with travel images. |
| Multiplayer | Multiple participants supply indispensable state and the outcome cannot be demonstrated as a single-user tool. | A shared dashboard, chat room, or multiplayer skin over a solo model. |
| Food | Food intake, preparation, allocation, or handling is the user’s actual job and the outcome affects food. | A generic scheduling/identity tool with restaurant examples. |

## Finalist implications

- **Ask Once** is an unusually strong Food case because label evidence, constraints, question, and allocation all directly concern food handling.
- **CycleClear** and **Bracket Patch** fit Optimization only when their objective/constraint delta is on screen.
- **Airlock** and **StaleMap** fit Traveling only when the action/route changes in a disruption or accessibility context.
- **Triangulate** and **FairTable** fit Multiplayer only when distinct participants’ inputs are required and visibly change shared state.
- **Sizzle Oracle** fits Food, but must avoid shrinking into generic voice assistance.

## Binding safeguards

1. Every final presentation starts with one sentence that names the track user and the track outcome, before discussing technology.
2. Every finalist must show a track-specific counterfactual: remove the core mechanism and a Food allocation, travel decision, group state, or optimization objective gets worse.
3. The submission’s required 50-word track explanation must reuse the product’s main sentence; a separate justification is evidence of cosmetic fit.
