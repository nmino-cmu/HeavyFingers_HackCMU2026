# Ninety-second demo simulations for every semifinalist

Each simulation is a judge-facing proof path, not a product tour. The first ten seconds must show the painful before-state. The 90-second sequence is the core of a timed 2:35–2:45 presentation: 15 seconds problem, 90 seconds proof, 35–45 seconds technical/sponsor evidence, 20 seconds track/usefulness close, and interruption buffer.

| Candidate | 0–10s: hook | 10–25s: evidence | 25–50s: turning point | 50–75s: proof | 75–90s: close | Judge critique to preempt |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Ask Once** | Photo of label; only 1/4 allocations is policy-eligible. | Gemini evidence graph marks sauce ingredient uncertain. | The app selects “Does it contain sesame?” rather than guessing. | Staff confirms; conservative matching flips to 4/4 and logs evidence. | “We do not certify food; we ask the highest-value human question.” | “Food matching exists.” Answer with the intake-clarification transformation and no safety claim. |
| **CycleClear** | Four people each have the wrong scarce slot; bilateral swaps fail. | Graph highlights a feasible 4-cycle and constraints. | Four approvals are collected; one devnet settlement moves all rights. | A fifth demo declines and no one loses their original right. | Explorer signature plus “all move or nobody moves.” | “Why Solana?” Show atomic settlement, not a database. |
| **Airlock** | Stranded traveler grants search/hold but not cancel. | Permission passport shows action boundaries. | Agent holds a replacement but cancellation gets a real denial. | Traveler approves; protected mock-airline action succeeds and audit appears. | “Help without handing over the keys.” | “Just RBAC.” Show Auth0-protected request and approval/audit. |
| **Triangulate** | Three teammates make conflicting photo/audio/text claims. | Claim graph exposes contradiction and source links. | App asks one teammate for the discriminating photo. | New evidence changes shared claim confidence in every view. | “We assign verification; we do not summarize rumors.” | “LLM wrapper.” Keep information-gain choice and evidence state visible. |
| **StaleMap** | Step-free route is green. | Existing report is visibly old; new outage report arrives. | Freshness/confidence turn red and path reroutes. | Restoration photo changes confidence and route again; history persists. | “Accessibility is evidence with an expiration date.” | “Accessibility maps exist.” Point to rerouting from conflict/decay. |
| **Bracket Patch** | Photograph a tangled sticky-note plan with a hidden impossibility. | Typed constraints and one red unsat core appear. | One tap proposes a minimal two-task patch. | The board animates from impossible to feasible; explanation names why. | “Point at the impossible plan; see the smallest fix.” | “OCR scheduler.” The photo-to-unsat-to-patch path is the proof. |
| **Sizzle Oracle** | Pan waveform begins low; cook is busy. | Threshold/state trace identifies a real acoustic change. | One concise ElevenLabs interruption says what to do. | Cook confirms by voice; state becomes handled; noise clip does nothing. | “The pan speaks before the mistake.” | “Voice cooking exists.” Focus on sound-triggered intervention. |
| **FairTable** | Four diners privately set constraints without social pressure. | Commit dots lock before any preferences reveal. | A late edit attempt becomes a new commitment and is excluded. | Reveal computes result plus aggregate reasons, hiding individual inputs. | “A fair choice without forcing disclosure.” | “Why a chain?” Demonstrate the exact manipulation it prevents. |
| **Resilience Canvas** | A sunny-day delivery plan looks optimal. | Road closure/no-show is dropped into the map. | Remote scenario meter runs several failure worlds. | Robust allocation loses fewer deliveries than baseline; job evidence appears. | “Rehearse failure, not just the happy path.” | “Vultr is hosting.” Show the computation changing allocation. |
| **RumorClock** | Shared claim is verified, then its timer visibly decays. | Separate browser shows same pending stale status. | A teammate supplies evidence. | Both views refresh to supported status with source/time. | “Shared truth decays unless someone renews it.” | “Colorful shared doc.” Emphasize cross-user, time-based state. |
| **BatchBraid** | Three batches begin together but later taste differs. | Photos, temperatures, observations braid in time. | Earliest divergence lights up. | App proposes one controlled next trial and labels it a hypothesis. | “Find the moment they stopped being the same.” | “Just a chart.” The next experimental action must be clear. |
| **Allergrant** | Diner shares a minimal stated constraint, not health record. | Kitchen has no access before authorization. | Diner grants a narrow, expiring read; kitchen acknowledges. | Time expiry produces actual protected-read denial and audit. | “The kitchen needs a constraint, not a medical file.” | “Safety guarantee or login demo.” State the limits and show revocation. |

## Presentation failure drills

| Failure during judging | Recovery sentence | Visual to keep on screen |
|---|---|---|
| Sponsor API slows or fails | “Here is the raw, source-linked response captured from the same fixture; the optimization and policy transition remain live.” | Original input, schema response, and state transition. |
| A judge questions novelty | “We are not claiming the broad category is new. The claim is this observable interaction, which the demo just showed.” | Before/after state, not a market-size slide. |
| A judge questions sponsor use | “Remove this sponsor primitive and this visible property disappears.” | Denied API call, transaction signature, model evidence, Atlas event, or remote-job result. |
| A judge questions safety | “We deliberately withhold uncertain conclusions and require a human-confirmed recorded fact.” | Uncertainty state and refusal path. |
| Time is cut to 45 seconds | Start at the before-state, show one turning point, then finish with the proof artifact. | The screen where the state visibly changes. |

## Independent demo-critic pass

This pass applies the same exhausted-judge questions to every semifinalist. “Stop?” means the first ten seconds are likely to command attention. “Remove” identifies the first tempting feature that should be cut.

| Candidate | Stop? / understand? / remember? | What needs too much explanation or sounds fake | Remove first | Sponsor visibility |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Ask Once | Yes / yes / yes: one answer changes four outcomes. | “Safely matched” can sound like a certification. | Marketplace, routing, medical language. | Gemini evidence JSON and visible uncertainty graph. |
| CycleClear | Yes / mostly / yes: all-or-nothing movement is memorable. | Why a blockchain is needed. | Prices, tokens, open exchange. | Actual devnet signature and reject branch. |
| Airlock | Yes / yes / yes if denial comes first. | “Agent” rhetoric and real-airline claims. | Chat/interface decoration and external OAuth. | Protected API denial, approval, audit. |
| Triangulate | Maybe / yes after one sentence / likely. | Confidence score can look invented. | Extra incident workflow. | Source-linked Gemini claims and requested observation. |
| StaleMap | Yes / yes / yes: green-to-red route change. | Map accuracy claims beyond fixture. | Full-map/search UI. | Atlas-backed evidence event and recomputation. |
| Bracket Patch | Yes / yes / yes: red conflict to repaired board. | Broad OCR promise. | General calendar and collaboration. | Gemini JSON plus deterministic solver repair. |
| Sizzle Oracle | Yes / yes / likely, if audio works. | “AI knows cooking” is overclaiming. | Recipes, meal planning, free-form chat. | ElevenLabs intervention/confirmation is audible. |
| FairTable | Maybe / yes after attack / less likely. | Privacy/the chain can become jargon. | Restaurant discovery and rewards. | Commit/reveal proof prevents a visible edit. |
| Resilience Canvas | Yes / mostly / likely. | Thousands of worlds can sound theatrical. | Map fidelity and fleet integrations. | Remote job output changes allocation. |
| RumorClock | Maybe / yes / medium. | Claim decay can feel arbitrary. | Chat and generic knowledge graph. | Atlas event changes a second screen. |
| BatchBraid | Maybe / yes / medium. | Causation claim from observations. | Recipe library and long timelines. | Atlas event history underpins aligned evidence. |
| Allergrant | Yes / yes / medium. | Health/safety implication and identity jargon. | Profiles, menus, recommendations. | Auth0 protected-read and actual expiry denial. |
