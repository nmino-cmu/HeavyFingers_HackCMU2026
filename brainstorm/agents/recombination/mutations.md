## Mutation hygiene

No mutation changes the selected track or adds a secondary sponsor. “Differentiated” means the identified interaction has a defensible narrow boundary; it does not mean the market or academic literature has been exhaustively cleared. The final jury therefore scores novelty alongside a required on-screen proof, rather than awarding it on a name alone.
