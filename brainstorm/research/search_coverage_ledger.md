## Rejection record for Food × Solana

The gap is deliberate. A conditional pickup pledge / no-show handoff was considered as a mechanism, but it sat too close to existing food-rescue matching and coordination products and introduced wallet friction without beating the selected food-intake demo. The food-rescue prior-art evidence is in [prior_art/decision_evidence.md](prior_art/decision_evidence.md). No weak Food × Solana card was added merely to complete the grid.

## Quality-over-volume waiver

The task sets a ceiling of 100 surviving candidates, not a quota or a per-track minimum. The completed database retains 34 cards after screening. Its distribution is Optimization 7, Traveling 7, Multiplayer 8, and Food 12. The coverage audit correctly notes that this does not prove broad generation by count alone; the per-candidate mechanism record in [../results/candidate_provenance.md](../results/candidate_provenance.md) and the raw ideation archive make the explored surface auditable.

## Anti-convergence rule

Fingerprint a candidate as:

> user job + input dependency + core mechanism + irreversible/shared state transition + judge-visible proof

At most two semifinalists may share a fingerprint family, and only one may be the final recommendation. Ask Once is the selected representative of the uncertainty-to-clarification family. Triangulate, StaleMap, and Bracket Patch remain comparison controls rather than co-recommendations.
