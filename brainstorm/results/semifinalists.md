## Narrowing decisions

- The final battle-card name **Airlock** is a presentation mutation of Proxy Passport. The underlying candidate and pair remain T1, Traveling × Auth0.
- **Bracket Patch** is a presentation mutation of ConstraintLens. The underlying candidate and pair remain O2, Optimization × Gemini.
- Ask Once is deliberately framed as a tool for staff using controlled policy rules. It must never infer an allergen, certify food, or promise safety.
- Six candidates were rejected before this set because they were either materially overlapped by existing products or lacked a proof-bearing sponsor role: T6, M5, F5, F10, F11, and F12.

## Semifinal selection model

The semifinal slate is a Pareto set across usefulness, technical difficulty, demo transformation, track fit, sponsor centrality, feasibility, novelty, and failure risk. A high score in a single category cannot rescue a candidate that fails its first-hours proof gate.
