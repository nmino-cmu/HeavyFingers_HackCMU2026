Each was attacked in seven roles: strongest advocate, HackCMU judge, sponsor judge, systems engineer, product skeptic, novelty researcher, and ruthless demo breaker. A candidate survives only if the same observable event answers the strongest attack across roles.

## Immediate attrition before the 28

T6 RepairRelay, M5 ManySight, F5 ChillChain, F10 MiseStorm, F11 Ferment Falsifier, and F12 Hot Together were removed because their clearest demo either duplicated an existing product category or could not show a sponsor-central state transition. They remain in the JSON database for auditability.

## Cross-role conclusions

- **Sponsor judges** favored O1, T1, M8, and F13 only if their proof is irreducible to the sponsor integration.
- **HackCMU judges** favored F13, O2, T4, and M1 for an understandable before/after state change.
- **Systems reviewers** downgraded O1 and T1 for integration fragility despite their high upside.
- **Novelty reviewers** downgraded voice cooking, general food-rescue, generic itinerary, and board-game-assistant shapes using the linked evidence ledger.
- **Demo breakers** found that every finalist can fail within the first two hours. The build simulations define the required fallback for that reason.
