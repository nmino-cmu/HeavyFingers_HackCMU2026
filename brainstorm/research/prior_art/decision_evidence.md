# Prior-art evidence ledger

This ledger records the closest things found for the serious pair candidates and the attractive concepts that were killed. It distinguishes a product's existence from a material overlap. A label of DIFFERENTIATED means the candidate may remain only if it visibly demonstrates the stated distinction; it is not a novelty claim.

| Candidate / mechanism | Closest evidence | Classification | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Proxy Passport** — a traveler grants an AI/companion narrow, expiring itinerary and booking authority | Auth0 itself documents AI-agent delegated authorization, Token Vault, and asynchronous approval; travel-agency tools offer role permissions; [Loyalty Click](https://www.loyclick.com/agentic-travel-simulator) shows agent travel mandates. | DIFFERENTIATED | Retain only as a consumer-facing, per-trip permission contract with a visual allow/deny proof. Do not claim to invent agent authorization or travel delegation. |
| **LiftLine** — fresh, confidence-weighted reports of elevator / entrance status immediately alter an accessible route | [AccessNow](https://accessnow.com/) and [Wheelmap](https://wheelmap.org/) are established accessibility maps; transit agencies publish elevator alerts. | DIFFERENTIATED | Retain only if its core is evidence expiry, conflicting-report confidence, and a route recomputation caused by a live report—not a directory of accessible places. |
| **FairTable** — secret group dining constraints/rankings, verifiable commitment, and deterministic group result | Secret-ballot and private social-choice work exists, and generic group-poll products exist. [Privacy-aware group decision work](https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.08156) identifies the same privacy/utility tension; Solana's official prize specifically supports social/consumer and high-frequency transactions. | DIFFERENTIATED | Retain if the devnet commit/reveal is necessary to prevent preference changes after others reveal, and if the output reveals only the group choice. Do not pitch it as a polling app. |
| **FlowState** — camera-calibrated crowd-flow counterfactual simulator | Real-time crowd simulations and camera/phone-fed crowd platforms have substantial research history: [micro-modelling](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S219643862500734X), [Kobe city twin](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10015-024-00941-y), [participatory crowd management](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13174-015-0040-6). | CROWDED | Retain only as a lower-ranked technical demo if it narrows to a small venue and proves a distinctive zero-setup calibration interaction. It cannot claim to be the first real-time crowd digital twin. |
| **Bottleneck Lens** — phone video becomes a process graph/time-study optimizer | [Process Study](https://process.study/), [SHOWHOW](https://showhow.emplif.ai/en), and [Proxima](https://proxima.vision/) already turn work videos into time studies, process steps, or video process mining. | ALREADY EXISTS | Kill. Changing the model/provider would not create a material new product. |
| **Kitchen Cadence** — hands-free cooking voice assistant | [AskChef](https://www.ask-chef.com/) and [Heard Recipes](https://heardrecipes.com/) already offer voice-first, hands-free cooking help; research prototypes such as [VoiceCookingAssistant](https://aclanthology.org/2021.icnlsp-1.30/) cover the mechanism. | ALREADY EXISTS | Kill. A different expressive voice does not change the central experience enough. |
| **Food-rescue matching** — realtime donor/driver/recipient optimization | [RescueRoute](https://rescueroute.org/), [Replate](https://blog.replate.org/blog/a-major-milestone-replate-receives-patent-for-food-recovery-technology), [Knead](https://kneadtech.com/), and [ZeroWasteLink](https://github.com/parthksingh1/ZeroWasteLink) already match, route, and coordinate surplus food in realtime. | ALREADY EXISTS | Kill. The market includes mature/recent implementations with the exact multi-sided loop. |
| **RuleLens** — camera board-game assistant/referee | [Board Law](https://ai.google.dev/competition/projects/board-law), [Start2Play](https://www.start2play.app/), [KnightVision](https://www.knightvision.app/), and the older [Kwyjibo](https://brm.io/kwyjibo/) cover camera identification, rule assistance, and live board tracking. | CROWDED | Kill for this event. A constrained custom game could work as entertainment, but it has weak usefulness and poor prize advantage. |

| **Generic visual food-recognition / nutrition assistant** | Large visual-food-recognition literature exists, including [Large Scale Visual Food Recognition](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.16107), plus many commercial scan-and-log tools. | CROWDED | Kill unless the core changes from identification to a narrow interaction that no product provides. |
| **Generic board-game rules chatbot** | [Board Game Genie](https://www.boardgenie.app/), [Game Guru](https://gameguruapp.com/), and [Ludomentor](https://ludomentor.com/) already offer rulebook-grounded assistants. | ALREADY EXISTS | Kill. |
| **Generic travel translation/voice concierge** | Existing translation apps and ElevenLabs's own examples make this a crowded category. | CROWDED | Kill without a distinct non-textual, interruption-critical task. |

## How this affects selection

The evidence reduces the apparent strength of camera-process optimization, food rescue, voice cooking, camera board-game assistance, generic itinerary generation, and visual food scans. It increases the relative value of three candidates whose central interactions are not already supplied as a polished product: per-trip agent delegation, evidence-decaying accessible navigation, and cryptographically fair private group choice. Those three still carry meaningful risk and must not be described as wholly unprecedented.
